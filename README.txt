RUBAYED DETAILING WEBSITE
=========================
Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

UPLOAD:
Upload all three files to your web hosting public_html/www folder. index.html is the homepage.

BEFORE GOING LIVE:
1. Replace +81 XX-XXXX-XXXX in index.html with your real phone.
2. Replace the WhatsApp number in the wa.me link with your real number (country code, no + or spaces).
3. Replace "Japan — add your workshop address" with your address.
4. Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com in script.js with your business email.
5. Replace the demo gallery backgrounds with your own vehicle photos if desired.

The contact form opens the visitor's email application; no server/backend is required.
